Orpheus is push-to-talk dictation: press a hotkey, talk, and the text is
typed into the window you are working in. Here is everything it does on
this PC.

WHAT IT CHANGES

- It installs for you only, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Orpheus. No
  administrator rights, nothing for other users.
- It adds "Orpheus" and "Orpheus settings" to the Start menu.
- If you keep "Start Orpheus when I sign in to Windows" ticked on the next
  page, it adds itself to your startup apps. You can turn that off in
  Orpheus settings or in Task Manager > Startup apps.
- While it runs it watches key presses only to spot its hotkey (Win+H by
  default, which takes that key over from Windows voice typing) and keeps
  none of them. It pastes the text with Ctrl+V and reads the clipboard
  only to put back what was on it.

WHAT IT SENDS

- Your recordings go to the transcription server you set in Orpheus
  settings, and nowhere else. Until you set one, that is this PC
  (localhost). Orpheus has no telemetry, no accounts and no update check.

UNINSTALLING

Settings > Apps > Orpheus > Uninstall removes the program and its startup
entry. Your settings (%APPDATA%\Orpheus) and any recordings saved after a
failed transcription (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Orpheus\failed) stay until you delete
them.
